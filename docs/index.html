TAPEWAVE

The Winamp spirit, for the internet age.

An open-source music player that plays your files, your radio and your podcasts. Built in Rust with a native Windows x64 interface. No WebView2 or browser frontend.

# What it plays

Open formats and open streams. No account, no ads, no ripping.

working now

Your local music

MP3, FLAC, OGG/Vorbis, WAV and AAC/MP4, played straight from your disk.

working now

Internet radio

Paste any direct audio stream URL and it plays. Icecast and SHOUTcast-style streams both work.

working now

Podcasts

Drop in an RSS or Atom feed and pick an episode. Audio comes straight from the publisher.

planned

Spotify & YouTube Music

Later, as embedded sources. Their players, their audio, so no EQ or visualizer for those.

# Roadmap

Where it's headed, in order.

  1. Playback coreLocal files, radio streams, podcast feeds, pause, stop and volume. Shipping now.
  2. PlaylistsM3U and PLS import and export, plus podcast subscriptions.
  3. EQ and spectrum visualizerA 10-band equalizer and the signature Winamp-style visualizer.
  4. Classic skinsLoad original Winamp .wsz skins, and make your own.
  5. Native platformsWindows x64 first. The independent Rust engine leaves room for other native platforms later.
  6. Embedded servicesSpotify and YouTube Music as sources, controlled through their official players.

# Build it

Early software. You need Windows 10/11 x64, stable Rust and Visual Studio C++ Build Tools with a Windows SDK. No WebView2 required. Installer and portable ZIP packaging are included.

Run from source 
git clone https://github.com/rahuling/tapewave
cd tapewave
cargo run --target x86_64-pc-windows-msvc
# Package with Inno Setup 6 installed:
./scripts/build-windows.ps1 -Installer
